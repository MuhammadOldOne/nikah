## 1. Fiqh of online matchmaking: communication, wali, mahram/third party, khalwa, nazar; Russian muftiates and major fatwa bodies

### Takeaway
Mainstream bodies are in broad agreement on four points:
- Seeking a spouse online is permissible in principle.
- Contact should have a marriage purpose, stay limited to what is needed, avoid flirtation, and involve the family or wali.

They split on three questions:
- **Unchaperoned chat.** SeekersGuidance (Shafi'i section) allows chat without a chaperone, though it calls a chaperone "superior". IslamQA's answer 13791 allows it with conditions. Askimam (Hanafi) and some Islamweb fatwas do not allow it.
- **Whether a private chat counts as khalwa.** IslamQA says it is not technically khalwa but is still disallowed as a means to fitnah.
- **The wali's role.** In the Hanafi school an adult woman's own contract is valid but discouraged; in the Shafi'i school the wali is a condition of validity.

Russian muftiates have generally welcomed matchmaking with a family purpose. Several prefer that religious bodies run or supervise it. No muftiate fatwa specifically on marriage apps was found.

### Cited Findings

**Communication with a prospective spouse online (international fatwa bodies)**
- **IslamQA.info, answer 212478 (strict).** Private chat between a man and a non-mahram woman is not allowed in principle because it "opens the door to evil and fitnah". A suitor should approach the woman's guardian and then check his character by permissible means. — [IslamQA 212478](https://islamqa.info/en/answers/212478)
- **IslamQA.info, answer 13791 (conditional).** Speaking with a non-mahram woman for marriage is allowed if there is no khalwa, the talk stays on permissible topics, there is no fitnah and no soft speech. Phone is better than meeting in person, and written contact (letters, email) is better still. It cites Shaykh al-Fawzan's preference that the man speak to the guardian rather than to the woman. — [IslamQA 13791](https://islamqa.info/en/13791)
- **IslamQA, answer 277583 (khalwa question).** A private chat between the sexes on social media "does not come under the heading of the khulwah that is forbidden". It is still disallowed because it is a means that leads to fitnah. — [IslamQA 277583](https://islamqa.info/en/answers/277583/does-chatting-between-the-sexes-on-social-media-sites-come-under-the-heading-of-khulwah-that-is-forbidden)
- **IslamQA, answer 2105.** Once a valid nikah contract exists, the couple may talk freely, even before consummation. An "online engagement" with no contract is different. — [IslamQA 2105](https://islamqa.com/en/answers/2105)
- **Islamweb, fatwa 85035 (strict).** Private online talk for marriage is not allowed. The prospective spouse's character should be investigated through family and the wali. — [Islamweb 85035](https://www.islamweb.net/en/fatwa/85035/chatting-on-the-internet-for-the-sake-of-marriage)
- **Islamweb, fatwa 398290 (need-based).** Talking to a non-mahram woman is permissible "when needed" and when one is secure from temptation, "only according to the need and without being in seclusion with her". Per the snippet, online conversation "does not require a group". — [Islamweb 398290](https://www.islamweb.net/en/fatwa/398290/chatting-with-a-non-mahram-woman-when-needed); see also [Islamweb 85313](https://www.islamweb.net/en/fatwa/85313/sending-emails-and-chatting-a-non-mahram)
- **SeekersGuidance (filed in IslamQA.org's Shafi'i section).** "It is permissible to speak to another for the intention of marriage through messaging or email without a chaperone. It would be superior to have a chaperone added to the conversation but it is not obligatory." The answer advises involving parents as things progress. A related SeekersGuidance answer notes that Muzmatch (now Muzz) chats "can be chaperoned… by adding the chaperone's email". — [SeekersGuidance](https://seekersguidance.org/answers/modesty/chatting-on-a-muslim-matrimonial-website/); [IslamQA.org mirror](https://islamqa.org/shafii/seekersguidance-shafii/169571/chatting-on-a-muslim-matrimonial-website/)
- **Askimam (Hanafi, Darul Iftaa Mahmudiyyah line; strict).** A man may formally meet a potential spouse with her mahram present, but "you cannot keep meeting her and looking at her, and neither can you chat or text with her". Apps designed to encourage informal communication are "akin to dating and thus not permissible". — [Askimam Q45987](https://www.askimam.org/public/question_detail/45987)
- **IslamOnline fiqh committee (permissive in principle).** Marriage arranged through the internet "cannot be called forbidden", but Shariah ethics apply and a decision should not rest on online chat alone. A second answer says the information exchanged should stay confidential and pictures should be excluded. — [IslamOnline 1](https://fiqh.islamonline.net/en/trying-to-get-married-through-the-internet/); [IslamOnline 2](https://fiqh.islamonline.net/en/meeting-and-marrying-through-the-internet-permissible)
- **AMJA.** No AMJA fatwa specifically on marriage apps was found. At AMJA's 20th Annual Imams' Conference (Oct 2024), Dr. Asif Hirani presented a paper titled "Examination of contemporary gender interactions and pre-marital constraints". Per the snippet it notes that "some scholars believe that matrimonial apps should not allow communication between users…". The rest of the text was not retrieved. — [AMJA conference paper (PDF)](https://www.amjaonline.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Examination-of-contemporary-gender-interactions-and-pre-marital-constraints-Dr.-Asif-Hirani.docx.pdf)
- **Malaysian academic fatwa journal (USIM *Journal of Fatwa*).** It discusses "halal matchmaking and dating" and lists conditions. Per the snippet these include running the service through the woman's wali, and "when these conditions are met, matchmaking… can also be halal". — [USIM J. Fatwa](https://jfatwa.usim.edu.my/index.php/jfatwa/article/download/662/588/3294)
- **Dar al-Ifta al-Misriyya (Egypt).** In 2014 the press reported fatwas against selfies and online chat between the sexes. Headlines include "Egypt fatwa bans online chats between sexes". The full text and conditions were not retrieved. — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/world/mena/egypt-fatwa-bans-online-chats-between-sexes-1.1379233); [Newsweek](https://www.newsweek.com/egyptian-islamic-authority-issues-fatwas-against-selfies-and-chatting-online-%20267832). A separate Dar al-Ifta fatwa on suitors viewing photos is covered under nazar below.
- **Blog summary (low quality).** One marriage-app blog says "no unified fatwa" exists on Muslim marriage apps. It quotes a UK Darul Ifta answer that permits matrimonial sites only if they avoid private chat and involve the woman's male mahram. The citations are garbled. — [HealthyNikah blog](https://healthynikah.com/blog/muslim-marriage-apps/are-muslim-dating-apps-haram/)

**The wali (Hanafi vs Shafi'i), relevant to Tatars and Bashkirs (Hanafi) and the North Caucasus (largely Shafi'i)**
- **Hanafi, relied-upon position.** The marriage of a free, sane, adult woman without her wali's approval is valid if the husband is a suitable match (kuf'). — [Darul Iftaa via IslamQA.org](https://islamqa.org/hanafi/daruliftaa/7744/divorced-woman-marrying-without-her-guardians-approval/)
- **Hanafi, Zahir al-Riwaya.** In the Zahir al-Riwaya transmission, Abu Hanifa holds the contract valid "regardless of her being a virgin or divorcee, or whether or not she matches her spouse in kafa'ah". On that reading, a wali who objects must pursue the matter himself; the contract is not void. — [The Maydan](https://themaydan.com/2019/12/female-agency-in-marriage-in-the-%E1%B8%A5anafi-school-of-law-between-damascus-and-transoxiana/)
- **Hanafi: valid but blameworthy.** Even when the contract is valid, marrying without the wali's consent is "generally blameworthy and going against the Sunnah". — [Jamiatul Ulama KZN](https://jamiat.org.za/does-the-hanafi-school-encourage-marriage-without-the-guardians-wali-approval/)
- **Shafi'i.** The wali's participation is essential for validity: "even an adult woman cannot contract her own marriage without wali". This comes from a secondary summary, a judicial-exam notes page. — [TheLegalBridge](https://thelegalbridge.in/notes/guardian-wali-in-muslim-marriage/). SeekersGuidance has a Shafi'i-fiqh answer on the same question; its full text was not retrieved. — [SeekersGuidance](https://seekersguidance.org/answers/shafii-fiqh/is-it-valid-to-marry-without-the-presence-of-a-wali-guardian/)

**Nikah contract concluded online (relevant if the platform offers an "online nikah" feature)**
- **Muftiate of Kazakhstan (Hanafi), 2021.** A number of councils and scholars issued fatwas that online nikah is not permissible. The reason is the requirement that the spouses and witnesses be present in one sitting (majlis). — [muftyat.kz](https://www.muftyat.kz/ru/qa/qa-otbasy/2021-02-23/35090-dozvolen-li-onlain-nikakh/)
- **Islam-today.ru.** Presence of the parties in one sitting is the evidence that nikah by phone or internet is invalid. — [islam-today.ru](https://islam-today.ru/veroucenie/vopros-otvet/mozno-li-zaklucit-nikah-onlajn/)
- **Al-isnad.kz.** A nikah over Skype is invalid; offer and acceptance must take place with at least two witnesses physically present. — [al-isnad.kz](https://al-isnad.kz/fikh/dejstvitelen-li-nikah-zaklyuchennyj-po-skajpu)
- **Islam.global.** A remote nikah can be used to fake the guardian's consent. A "phone nikah" done out of convenience is invalid. — [islam.global](https://islam.global/semya/semeynaya-zhizn/nikakh-po-telefonu/)
- **Minority view.** Shaykh Ibn Jibrin, quoted on a forum (low-quality source), allowed a remote contract if the husband, wali and witnesses are all present, possibly over the internet. — [asar-forum](https://asar-forum.com/viewtopic.php?t=1866)

**Nazar: the Sunnah of looking at a prospective spouse**
- **The hadiths.**
  - Al-Mughira ibn Shu'ba was told to look at the woman he proposed to "because that is more likely to create love between you" (Abu Dawud, al-Tirmidhi, al-Nasa'i).
  - Jabir: the suitor may look at what would encourage him to marry her. Dar al-Ifta Egypt cites both in its fatwa allowing a man engaged to a veiled woman to see her photo. — [Dar al-Ifta Egypt fatwa 6140](https://www.dar-alifta.org/en/fatwa/details/6140/is-it-allowed-for-a-man-engaging-a-veiled-woman-to-see-her-photo-prior-to-marriag)
  - Abu Hurayra (Muslim 1424): "go and look at her, for there is something in the eyes of the Ansar". The Russian translation of Sahih Muslim titles the chapter "Encouraging the one who wishes to marry a woman to look at her face and hands". — [isnad.link (Sahih Muslim, ch. 12)](https://isnad.link/book/sahih-muslim/16-kniga-braka-hadisy-1400-1443/12-glava-pobuzhdenie-smotret-na-lico-i-kisti-ruk-zhenshiny-tomu-kto-zhelaet-na-nej-zhenitsya)
- **What may be seen (madhhab differences).**
  - Hanafis: face and hands. Abu Hanifa also allows the feet, since they are not 'awra.
  - Hanbalis and others: more, namely what she uncovers before mahrams.
  - Conditions: no seclusion (al-San'ani: in the presence of father, mother or another person) and no looking with desire (Ahmad). — [islam.global](https://islam.global/semya/semeynaya-zhizn/na-kakie-chasti-tela-nevesty-vprave-posmotret-muzhchina/); [soliha.com](https://soliha.com/fiqh/niqah_talaq/na-kakie-chasti-tela-zhenshhiny-dozvoleno-posmotret-pered-nikaxom); [Jordan Iftaa' Dept. fatwa 815](https://www.aliftaa.jo/research-fatwa-english/815/What-is-Permissible-for-the-Suitor-to-See-of-the-Woman-before-the-Wedding)
- **Faraz Rabbani (Russian translation, al-isnad.kz).** Looking at the face and hands is allowed only once the man has already resolved to marry. Removing hijab before a potential suitor is forbidden per the Sunni majority, "regardless of how close you are" to accepting. — [al-isnad.kz](https://al-isnad.kz/musulmanka/nemnogo-o-fotografiyah-zhenshhiny-bez-hid/)
- **Woman's permission.** Islamweb (per snippet) holds that the suitor's viewing of the woman or her picture is not conditional on her permission. — [Islamweb 313573](https://www.islamweb.net/en/fatwa/313573/giving-suitor-a-photograph-of-girl-he-wishes-to-propose-to). Photos are covered in section 2.

### Inferences
- The scholarly spectrum maps onto a product "strictness dial". Every position across these sources would accept the same core design:
  - marriage-only intent declared at signup;
  - no anonymous public browsing of women's images;
  - chat only after mutual interest;
  - an optional wali or chaperone that can be made mandatory per user;
  - content rules (no flirtation or intimate talk);
  - an early push toward family involvement and a supervised in-person meeting.
- Making the wali feature configurable per user accommodates both Hanafi users (adult woman may act herself, wali recommended) and Shafi'i users (wali required for the contract). Options could run from off, to read-only copy, to wali-mediated contact only.
- The platform should not offer an "online nikah contract". Hanafi bodies in the region (muftiates of Kazakhstan and Tatarstan-area sources) treat it as invalid. The platform's job should end at "families meet, then a nikah is held at a mosque or by an imam". Partnering with local imams or muftiate structures fits the Stavropol and DUM RF view that religious organisations should supervise matchmaking.
- The DUM RF polygyny episode shows that a polygyny filter or "second wife" mode carries legal and regulatory risk in Russia. The prosecutor's office intervened against even a theological opinion.
- For Chechen and Dagestani women, a leaked photo carries a real risk of social and physical harm. This is the strongest argument for photo-hidden-by-default, and it goes beyond religious preference.

- No primary-text fatwa was found from DUM RF, DUM RT, the Muftiate of Dagestan or the Muftiate of Chechnya specifically on marriage apps, online chat or photos in profiles. The search budget ran out before islamdag.ru, dumrt.ru and umma.ru could be checked directly. Recommend asking these bodies directly, or getting a written opinion (заключение) from a DUM RT or Dagestan fatwa department as a trust asset.
- Darul Uloom Deoband's own marriage-app fatwa (darulifta-deoband.com) was not retrieved. Only Deobandi-line answers mirrored on IslamQA.org were seen.

- The full text of the 2014 Egyptian Dar al-Ifta "online chat" fatwas, and whether they have been updated since, was not retrieved.
- Divorced and widowed women: no specific source was found on how the wali or consent rules change for them (e.g. the Shafi'i rule that a previously married woman must give explicit verbal consent). The only related point is the Maydan note that Abu Hanifa treats virgin and divorcee alike for validity.

## 2. Photos and images: women's photos on marriage sites, tasvir (including cartoon, stylized and AI images), blur vs stylization, nazar vs deception (gharar/tadlis)

### Takeaway
Two separate fiqh questions are involved.

**(a) Showing a woman's real photo.** Most bodies forbid or strongly discourage public display. Many allow a private, unaltered, modest photo shown only to a serious suitor as a fallback when an in-person look is not possible. IslamQA and Ibn Uthaymin are stricter about sending one.

**(b) Making an image of a living being.** Here views range widely:
- Darul Ifta Birmingham and others in the Deobandi line forbid any AI or cartoon avatar of oneself.
- Islamweb's preferred view is that photos and AI images are permissible.
- Jordan's Iftaa' Department (2025) permits AI photo-to-drawing conversion, explicitly provided it is not used for "deception".

No source says that a stylized avatar fulfils the Sunnah of nazar. The criteria scholars use ("real, unaltered", "shows reality") point the other way. Faceless or featureless depictions are acceptable even under the strict view.

### Cited Findings

**Posting women's photos on marriage sites**
- **Deobandi-line fatwa (Hanafi, mirrored on IslamQA.org).** Per the snippet: "Her picture should not be uploaded on the website, instead necessary information should be given. It is not right to give picture for a marriage proposal, neither it is lawful to publish it on website which can be seen by anyone. It is against the sanctity of women along with being against Shariah." — [IslamQA.org Hanafi – "using matrimonial websites"](https://islamqa.org/hanafi/muftionline/97218/using-matrimonial-websites/). The exact page for this quote was not pinned; other candidate pages are [157765](https://islamqa.org/hanafi/fatwaa-dot-com/157765/can-we-show-pictures-for-marriage-via-matrimonial-websites/) and [Albalagh 22374](https://islamqa.org/hanafi/albalagh/22374/giving-out-female-photograph-during-marriage-proposal/).
- **Another Hanafi answer on IslamQA.org.** It answers whether a man may post his sister's photo on a matrimonial site. It calls pictures "haraam and a curse", yet says posting is permissible "though it is not a respectable thing to do". This shows that strict Hanafi answers themselves are inconsistent. — [IslamQA.org Hanafi 157765](https://islamqa.org/hanafi/fatwaa-dot-com/157765/can-we-show-pictures-for-marriage-via-matrimonial-websites/) (exact page not pinned)
- **Askimam, answer 103496.** It addresses whether a bride's photo may be sent to the groom. — [Askimam 103496](https://islamqa.org/hanafi/askimam/103496/is-it-permissible-to-send-a-photo-of-a-bride-to-the-groom/) (the ruling's text was not retrieved)
- **IslamQA.info.** Marriage sites "should not show pictures of the women, because looking at the woman to whom one is proposing is only allowed for the suitor once he has resolved to marry her". Sites should also not describe a woman as if one could see her. — [IslamQA.info 4027](https://islamqa.info/en/answers/4027) (the answer number is a likely match, not confirmed)
- **IslamOnline.** Information should stay confidential and pictures should be excluded. — [IslamOnline](https://fiqh.islamonline.net/en/meeting-and-marrying-through-the-internet-permissible)
- **Volga and Stavropol imams.** As quoted in section 1, an imam says nazar of the face and hands is allowed, but a photo on social media "will be seen by everyone". — [Idel.Realii](https://www.idelreal.org/a/vip-tarif-bezlimit-eto-ne-haram-kak-musulmane-povolzhya-znakomyatsya-v-sotssetyah/33020235.html)

**A photo used for nazar (private, to a serious suitor)**
- **SeekersGuidance.** If a direct meeting is not possible and the woman is comfortable sending a photo to a trusted person, it is permissible, "provided the photo is real, unaltered, and fulfills the purpose of the suitor seeing her". The photo should be modest, with full hijab and no hair visible. — [SeekersGuidance](https://seekersguidance.org/answers/halal-and-haram/can-i-view-a-womans-picture-without-a-hijab-before-proposing-marriage/)
- **Islamweb 313573.** The picture should show the face and hands and what is normally uncovered before mahrams, such as the head and neck. "There is nothing wrong with looking repeatedly at it, but it is not permissible for the suitor to keep this picture." It discourages leaving a hard or soft copy with him. — [Islamweb 313573](https://www.islamweb.net/en/fatwa/313573/giving-suitor-a-photograph-of-girl-he-wishes-to-propose-to)
- **IslamQA 222753.** The basic principle is to look at the woman in person, since "a picture does not usually show reality". A photo is only a fallback for a serious suitor. It cites Ibn Uthaymin: "It is not allowed to send the woman's picture to the suitor", because of likely negative consequences. — [IslamQA 222753](https://islamqa.info/en/answers/222753/helping-the-suitor-to-see-a-picture-of-the-woman-to-whom-he-wants-to-propose); see also [IslamQA 132499](https://islamqa.info/en/answers/132499)
- **Dar al-Ifta Egypt, fatwa 6140.** It permits a man engaged to a veiled woman to see her photo before marriage, citing the Mughira and Jabir hadiths. — [Dar al-Ifta 6140](https://www.dar-alifta.org/en/fatwa/details/6140/is-it-allowed-for-a-man-engaging-a-veiled-woman-to-see-her-photo-prior-to-marriag)

**Deception (ghish/tadlis): editing, makeup, filters**
- **IslamQA 22845 ("Cheating").** Lists among deceptive practices in marriage families that "fill her face with all kinds of colours and dyes before a suitor sees her". It condemns this as cheating that destroys homes. — [IslamQA 22845](https://islamqa.info/en/answers/22845)
- **Ibn Uthaymin, via IslamQA 102369.** The woman should appear without cosmetics at the viewing, partly because the suitor "may see her as more beautiful than she really is" and be disappointed later. — [IslamQA 102369](https://islamqa.info/en/answers/102369)
- **Editing photos (Islamweb 333949, citing Ibn Uthaymin).** Once a person intervenes to alter or improve a photo, "the matter is no longer permissible". — [Islamweb 333949](https://www.islamweb.net/en/fatwa/333949/making-changes-to-photos-with-editing-tools)
- **Islamweb 516592.** Adopts the view that photographic images of living beings are permissible, while noting the disagreement. — [Islamweb 516592](https://www.islamweb.net/en/fatwa/516592/key-rulings-related-to-the-images-of-living-beings)
- **Snapchat-type face-changing filters.** Attributed to Shaykh al-Fawzan: "This is prohibited and from the plot of Shaytan." — [Darussaafi](https://darussaafi.com/?p=5381)

**Tasvir: photos, digital images, cartoons**
- **Muftiate of Dagestan (Shafi'i).** Fatwa "On the depiction of living beings": most Shafi'i scholars hold it forbidden to create complete figures of living beings as they exist in nature.
  - Not forbidden: images of nature (trees, mountains, cities), living beings depicted "in a form incompatible with life", and children's toys and dolls.
  - It is based on al-Malibari's *Fath al-Mu'in* (vol. 1, p. 492).
  - No separate muftiate fatwa on photography was found. — [muftiyatrd.ru](https://www.muftiyatrd.ru/fatawa/ob-izobrazhenii-zhivyh-sushchestv); [islamdag.ru](https://islamdag.ru/fatawa/49124)
- **Digital photo debate (Russian-language sources).**
  - The Permanent Committee (al-Lajna al-Da'ima) held that making and keeping photos of living beings is forbidden.
  - Others, including Egyptian mufti Muhammad Bakhit, held photography permissible as the "capture of a real image".
  - Some hold digital images permissible as "digital code". — [al-isnad.kz](https://al-isnad.kz/fikh/dozvoleny-li-tsifrovye-izobrazheniya-i-video-zhivyh-sushhestv/); [askimam.ru](https://askimam.ru/fatwa/islamskoe-pravo/dozvoleny-li-tsifrovye-izobrazheniya-i-video-zhivykh-sushchestv/); [Provereno.Media](https://provereno.media/blog/2021/08/27/pravda-li-chto-v-islame-zapresheno-izobrazhat-zhivyh-sushestv/)
- **Darul Uloom Karachi (Mufti Taqi Usmani line, Hanafi/Deobandi).** Live or recorded screen images are not prohibited tasvir unless printed in durable form. Printing is the dividing line. The position is contested within Deobandi circles, and some sources report the opposite attribution. The sources are secondary forum and Q&A pages. — [Darul Iftaa (daruliftaa.com)](https://daruliftaa.com/miscellaneous/photographs-please-clarify-your-position/); [Muftionline](https://muftionline.co.za/taxonomy/term/3056?page=11); [Muftisays forum](https://www.muftisays.com/forums/12-virtues/7601-the-prohibition-of-photography-.html?p=111839)
- **Cartoons, strict Hanafi (Askimam 81279, citing Radd al-Muhtar).** Drawing any tasvir, including cartoon characters, is impermissible. However, "if all the facial features… nose, mouth and eyes are removed, it is no longer considered a taswir". — [Askimam 81279](https://islamqa.org/hanafi/askimam/81279/is-it-permissible-to-draw-cartoon-characters/); also [Askimam 82970](https://islamqa.org/hanafi/askimam/82970/is-drawing-cartoons-impermissible/); [Darul Iftaa Birmingham on cartoons](https://islamqa.org/hanafi/daruliftaa-birmingham/244987/are-cartoons-like-hana-and-omar-permissible/)
- **Cartoons, Shafi'i (Jordan Iftaa').** Drawings of living beings with the eyes, nose and mouth erased are permissible. Many scholars permitted drawing beings that do not exist in reality, such as fictional characters. — [Jordan Iftaa via IslamQA.org](https://islamqa.org/shafii/darul-iftaa-jordan/227667/rules-of-drawing-cartoons/)
- **Islamweb.** The ruling on full-body drawings of animate beings is a matter of scholarly difference. — [Islamweb 448768](https://www.islamweb.net/en/fatwa/448768/drawing-imaginary-animate-beings-with-complete-body-form). Educational cartoons without music are fine. — [Islamweb 87864](https://www.islamweb.org/en/fatwa/87864/animated-tv-cartoons)

**AI-generated or AI-stylized images of a real person (the founder's "cartoon avatar" idea)**
- **Darul Ifta Birmingham (Hanafi/Deobandi), "Is it permissible to create an AI-generated 'avatar' of oneself?" — impermissible.** Creating or generating images of animate beings, "whether in cartoon form or as a realistic photo-like picture", is impermissible. The person who commands the AI is directly responsible. Using AI for a cartoon-like avatar of oneself is impermissible whatever the purpose. — [Darul Ifta Birmingham](https://daruliftabirmingham.co.uk/is-it-permissible-to-create-an-ai-generated-avatar-of-oneself/)
  - The same body applies this to AI images of real athletes: complete living images are impermissible; incomplete or non-living ones are treated differently. — [Birmingham: AI images of athletes](https://daruliftabirmingham.co.uk/the-ruling-on-using-ai-images-of-real-athletes/)
  - A search summary of another Birmingham page reads more leniently (the concern being tied to idolatry and immorality). This conflict was not resolved. — [Birmingham: AI images](https://daruliftabirmingham.co.uk/are-ai-generated-images-permissible/)
- **Muftisays.** AI images or avatars of humans are "not permissible as you are the cause of making such images". — [Muftisays](https://www.muftisays.com/qa/Halal-Haram/10944-making-ai-images-of-human-beings-or-maybe-making-different-avatars-is-permissible-or-not-/)
- **Fatwa.ca.** The user is the cause of the AI's output, or uses the AI merely as a tool. — [Fatwa.ca](https://fatwa.ca/artificial-intelligence-tool-ai/)
- **Islamweb.** The "preponderant" view is that AI-generated images "are permissible, and they are not included in the forbidden photography of animated beings". — [Islamweb 475069](https://islamweb.net/en/fatwa/475069/electronic-photography-through-artificial-intelligence); [Islamweb 528754](https://www.islamweb.net/en/fatwa/528754/ruling-on-ai-generated-images-using-music-and-earning-from-youtube)
- **Jordan General Iftaa' Department, fatwa no. 3989 (24 July 2025), converting photographs into drawings with AI.**
  - This is not the same as prohibited human-made tasweer; it is a digital transformation of an existing photo.
  - It is permitted if content and use do not conflict with Islamic rulings and ethics.
  - Conditions: no exposed 'awra, and the image must not be used for "lying, forgery, fraud or deception". — [aliftaa.jo 3989](https://www.aliftaa.jo/research-fatwa-english/3989/Ruling-on-Converting-Photographs-into-Drawings-Using-Artificial-Intelligence-Technology)
  - This is the most directly on-point fatwa found for the founder's idea.
- **Luton Muslims Journal (community Q&A).** Using one's own likeness with AI is permissible in principle, subject to no deception and no harm. Replicating another person's face or voice without permission violates people's rights. — [Luton Muslims Journal](https://lutonmuslimjournal.com/islamicqa/islamic-ruling-on-the-use-of-artificial-intelligence-for-voice-image-and-visual-representation/)
- **Dar al-Ifta Egypt, fatwa 24168.** Animating an AI image or video of a deceased person for comfort is allowed. It is "categorically prohibited" to make them appear to say what they never said. This is an analogous anti-misrepresentation principle. — [Dar al-Ifta 24168](https://www.dar-alifta.org/en/fatwa/details/24168/creating-an-ai-generated-video-of-a-deceased-person)

### Inferences
- **Does a stylized avatar fulfil nazar?** No source says it does. The criteria scholars use for photos in nazar point against it: "real, unaltered, fulfils the purpose" (SeekersGuidance) and "a picture does not usually show reality" (IslamQA). A cartoon or beautified avatar should therefore be presented as an icebreaker or personality signal, not as the nazar. The nazar still needs either a real-photo reveal to a specific serious suitor (with the woman's or wali's consent) or a supervised in-person or video viewing.
- **Deception risk.** Jordan Iftaa' explicitly conditions AI stylization on not being used for deception. The classical ghish rulings (makeup before the suitor's viewing) imply that a beautified avatar is a tadlis risk. To reduce it:
  - prevent beautification (no skin smoothing, slimming or skin lightening);
  - label the image clearly as "stylized illustration, not a photo";
  - never let a stylized image replace the real-photo or in-person step.
- **Paradox for the strictest audience.** To Deobandi-line users (Birmingham, Askimam), an AI cartoon of oneself is more objectionable than having no image, because it is an act of tasvir by the user. A faceless or featureless avatar, such as a silhouette, a hijab or kufi pictogram, or a figure without eyes, nose and mouth, fits the strict Hanafi exception (Askimam 81279), the Shafi'i Jordan Iftaa' rule and the Dagestan muftiate's "form incompatible with life" exception. It is the only image option that fits every position found.
- **A possible tiered image model:**
  - Tier 0: no image, or an abstract or featureless avatar (acceptable to all).
  - Tier 1: an optional AI-stylized portrait, labelled and non-beautified (acceptable to Islamweb and Jordan-type views, not to Birmingham-type views).
  - Tier 2: a real photo, hidden by default and revealed per match by the woman (or her wali), view-only and not storable, matching Islamweb's "may look, must not keep".
- **Russian context.** Tatar and Bashkir audiences (Hanafi, often Deobandi- or Turkish-influenced) and Dagestani and Chechen audiences (Shafi'i, socially conservative) are likely to see real-photo publication as the bigger problem. Hidden photos plus featureless avatars are the safest default. This is an inference; no survey of Russian Muslims on this was found.

### Gaps
- No fatwa was found that directly addresses "AI cartoon avatar instead of a photo on a marriage platform", or that rules on whether a stylized likeness satisfies nazar. Recommend asking DUM RT and Muftiate of Dagestan fatwa departments a precise written question.
- Darul Uloom Deoband's own current position on digital images and AI was not retrieved, only Birmingham and Karachi-line views.
- No Russian muftiate position on AI-generated images was found.

## 3. Product patterns on existing Muslim marriage platforms

### Takeaway
The market has settled on a consistent set of features:
- photos blurred or hidden, revealed per match;
- selfie verification reviewed by women;
- screenshot blocking (fully possible on Android; detect-and-warn only on iOS);
- chat only after a mutual match;
- an optional wali or chaperone, as weekly transcripts (Muzz), real-time copy (Pure Matrimony) or a read-only invite (Salams);
- moderation and reporting.

Fake profiles and scams remain the top user complaint despite verification. No major Muslim app was found using AI avatars. Some explicitly market themselves as filtering AI-generated profiles.

### Cited Findings

**Muzz (largest player; Wikipedia: 21M+ members, other reports say 15M+ or 17M)**
- **Chaperone/wali, as a help-centre feature.** "Chaperone Mode enables you to share weekly transcripts of your chats on Muzz with a wali." — [Muzz Help: How is Muzz halal](https://muzz.com/help/getting-started/how-is-muzz-halal) (per search summary)
- **Chaperone/wali, as press-described.** A 2024 company interview says the chaperone has "access to all of your activity in the app", and both people can see that a chaperone is present. — [ProPakistani](https://propakistani.pk/2024/05/17/muzz-is-putting-rishta-aunties-out-of-business/)
- **India launch (22 Apr 2026).** Coverage describes a "Wali" option that lets users invite a trusted family member into conversations. No one is required to invite a wali. — [Global Dating Insights](https://www.globaldatinginsights.com/featured/muzz-officially-launches-in-indias-muslim-marriage-market/)
- The weekly-transcript and live-access descriptions conflict, or reflect different versions of the feature.
- **Photo blur.** Available only to women, via "Settings > Profile Photo Visibility > Blur my photos", with reveal to a specific match from the chat menu. "At the moment, men aren't able to blur their profile photos." — [Muzz Help: why can't I blur](https://muzz.com/us/en/help/safety-and-privacy/why-cant-i-hide-my-photos-on-my-profile/); [Muzz Help: safety](https://muzz.com/us/en/help/safety-and-privacy/how-does-muzz-keep-users-safe/)

  - Wikipedia says Muzz originally blurred all profile pictures. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muzz_(dating_app))
- **Selfie verification.** Mandatory: "Failure to upload a valid verification can result in a permanent ban." The selfie is compared with the profile photo, "only reviewed by our female Trust & Safety team, so no man will ever see it", and never shown on the profile. Verification in hijab or niqab is supported. — [Muzz Help: safety](https://muzz.com/us/en/help/safety-and-privacy/how-does-muzz-keep-users-safe/); [Muzz Help: hijab/niqab](https://muzz.com/us/en/help/getting-started/can-i-selfie-verify-my-muzz-profile-in-hijab-or-niqab/)

- **Scams.** Muzz publishes scam-spotting guidance. — [Muzz blog](https://muzz.com/blog/muzz/how-to-spot-a-scammer-on-a-muslim-marriage-app/). Trustpilot reviewers still report "far too many bots and fake profiles" and opaque permanent bans (anecdotal). — [Trustpilot](https://ca.trustpilot.com/review/muzz.com)
- **AI.** No reliable report of a Muzz AI bio-writer or AI matchmaker launch was found. Vendor blogs vaguely mention "AI features". — [GDI](https://www.globaldatinginsights.com/featured/muzz-officially-launches-in-indias-muslim-marriage-market/)

**Others and adjacent**
- **NikahPlus** claims its verification filters "fake and AI-generated profiles". — [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.nikkah.nikka7)
- **Qaboolify** labels its own AI features ("all AI-generated content is clearly labelled") and uses ID verification plus voice intros to show that a real person is behind each profile. — [Qaboolify](https://qaboolify.com/)
- **Sunnah Match** runs a public showcase with no profile photos; photo sharing is listed as "coming soon". — [Sunnah Match](https://www.sunnahmatch.com/)

- **Screenshot blocking precedent (Badoo, 2021).** Android users cannot take screenshots or screen recordings. iOS cannot fully block them, so iOS users get a warning. — [Business Insider India](https://businessinsider.in/science/health/news/a-dating-app-has-banned-users-from-taking-screenshots-to-keep-messages-private/amp_articleshow/83992199.cms)

### Inferences
- **Patterns proven in market** (Muzz, Salams, Pure Matrimony, Hawaya): photo blur or hiding with per-match reveal; mandatory selfie verification reviewed by women; screenshot blocking on Android and warnings on iOS; chat only after mutual match; optional wali access (weekly transcript, real-time copy, or read-only invite); no contact-detail exchange until a later stage; message monitoring and reporting.
- **Gaps a Russian Sharia-first product could fill:**
  - **Photo hiding for both sexes.** Muzz offers blur to women only.
  - **Mandatory wali mode as a per-user option.** Useful for Shafi'i families or users who want stricter rules.
  - **Wali-to-wali channel.** No incumbent offering was found.
  - **Biodata/CV exchange.** A structured "анкета" PDF is common in South Asian rishta practice; no source found in this search.
  - **Time-boxed chat.** For example, a nudge to involve families after N days or N messages. The "N" would be a design choice; no evidence was found for any specific value.
- **Fake profiles and scams are the main trust failure across incumbents.** Verification alone is not enough. Possible additions are human moderation, rate limits on new accounts, and detection of keywords about money or crypto requests and moves off-platform.

### Gaps
- No sources were found on these patterns, which are only design ideas in this research:
  - women-first messaging in Muslim apps (Bumble-style);
  - limits on chat duration;
  - wali-to-wali communication;
  - matchmaker or moderator intermediaries in apps, as opposed to the offline DUM RF service;
  - biodata exchange.
- No sources were found on polygyny settings in existing apps. In Russia, see the DUM RF Dec 2024 episode in section 1.
- No sources were found on handling divorced or widowed users, beyond the general practice of marital-status filters, which was not cited.
- The current Muzz help-centre text could not be read in full; the domain was blocked. The conflict between weekly-transcript and live-access chaperone descriptions is unresolved.
- No independent data on fake-profile rates at any Muslim app was found; only anecdotes exist.

## 4. AI avatar / photo-stylization technology: tools, costs, privacy and law, risks, use by Muslim apps

### Takeaway
Identity-preserving stylization is mature and cheap. Hosted APIs for InstantID, PuLID, FLUX Kontext, GPT-Image or Gemini "Nano Banana" cost roughly $0.005–$0.17 per image, and typically about $0.02–$0.04. The main risks are:
- beautification and bias (the Lensa precedent: sexualized outputs, skin lightening);
- biometric and data-protection law (Russia's 572-FZ reportedly restricts biometric identification outside the state Unified Biometric System, the EBS; sending selfies to foreign APIs is a cross-border transfer);
- shutdown risk of avatar platforms (Ready Player Me went offline 31 Jan 2026).

No Muslim marriage app was found offering AI avatars, and there are no user or scholar reactions to cite.

### Cited Findings

**Identity-preserving methods (research)**
- **InstantID** is zero-shot identity-preserving generation from a single image, with no fine-tuning. It extends IP-Adapter-FaceID with a ControlNet on facial keypoints. Its authors report better fidelity than IP-Adapter-FaceID and PhotoMaker while keeping text editability. — [InstantID paper (arXiv 2401.07519)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2401.07519); [project page](https://instantid.github.io/)
- **Third-party benchmarks are mixed:**
  - FaceChain-FACT found InstantID had the largest drop in text alignment (CLIP-T) and "unsatisfactory" facial texture, while PhotoMaker ranked well on prompt following. — [FaceChain-FACT (arXiv 2410.12312)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2410.12312)
  - ConsistentID found PhotoMaker weaker on ID consistency and InstantID limited in pose flexibility. — [ConsistentID (arXiv 2404.16771)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2404.16771)
  - Architecture notes: PhotoMaker stacks CLIP features from multiple face images. — [Visual Persona (arXiv 2503.15406)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2503.15406)

**Hosted APIs and costs (aggregator figures, flagged as such)**
- **Replicate `zsxkib/instant-id-basic`.** Runs on an Nvidia A100 80GB, typically about 14 s per prediction. — [Replicate](https://replicate.com/zsxkib/instant-id-basic)
  - The A100 rate is about $0.0014/s per a third-party index. — [Flexprice](https://flexprice.io/pricing-index/replicate)
  - **Estimate (my own calculation, not a quoted price):** about $0.02 per image, excluding cold starts.
- **fal.ai.**
  - FLUX.1 Kontext [pro] image-to-image: $0.04 per image. — [fal model page](https://fal.ai/models/fal-ai/flux-pro/kontext)
  - PuLID-FLUX: about $0.035 per image; Kontext Max: about $0.08 (aggregator). — [PricePerToken](https://pricepertoken.com/fal-ai-pricing)
  - Failed requests (HTTP 5xx) and cold starts are reportedly not billed (aggregator).
- **OpenAI image API.**
  - gpt-image-1 at 1024²: about $0.011 / $0.042 / $0.167 for low / medium / high quality. Aggregators say it was retired from the API on 23 Oct 2026.
  - gpt-image-1-mini: about $0.005 / $0.011 / $0.036.
  - gpt-image-1.5: about $0.009 / $0.034 / $0.133 (as of 24 Mar 2026). Edits add input-image tokens at about $8/M.
  - All are aggregator figures; verify on openai.com/pricing. — [PricePerToken](https://pricepertoken.com/gpt-image-pricing); [AIFreeAPI](https://www.aifreeapi.com/en/posts/gpt-image-1-5-pricing-api)
- **Google Gemini 2.5 Flash Image ("Nano Banana").** $0.039 per image at about 1 MP; Batch $0.0195. It was reportedly replaced by "Nano Banana 2" as the Gemini app default in Feb 2026 but remains on the API. — [PricePerToken](https://pricepertoken.com/pricing-page/model/google-gemini-2.5-flash-image)
- **Ready-made avatar SDKs.**
  - Ready Player Me was acquired by Netflix in Dec 2025. Its public avatar creator and developer APIs went offline on 31 Jan 2026, and users could not export avatars. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ready_Player_Me); [Avatar SDK blog](https://avatarsdk.com/blog/2026/01/15/switch-from-ready-player-me-to-avatar-sdk-fast-familiar-production-ready/)
  - Alternatives named include Avatar SDK MetaPerson, Genies and Avaturn (mostly vendor-promoted). — [Genies blog](https://genies.com/blog/ready-player-me-discontinued-alternatives)
  - No public Bitmoji or Memoji API for third-party apps was found.

**Risks: the Lensa "Magic Avatars" precedent (Dec 2022–2023)**
- **Sexualized output.** An MIT Technology Review journalist reported that 16 of her 100 avatars were topless and 14 more were in skimpy clothing or sexualized poses. The output skewed toward women and Asian women. — [AI Incident Database #423](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/423/)
- **Skin lightening.** Women of colour reported their skin was lightened and their features "anglicized". — [AIID #423](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/423/)
- **Cause.** The app is built on Stable Diffusion, and the model's safety filter reportedly was not used. — [CyberGhost summary](https://www.cyberghostvpn.com/privacyhub/lensa-ai-app/)
- **Biometric lawsuit.** A class action alleged that Prisma Labs violated Illinois BIPA by capturing facial geometry from selfies without proper disclosure or a deletion policy, and by requiring access to all device photos. Prisma said photos are deleted after generation. The outcome was not found. — [Bloomberg Law](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/tech-and-telecom-law/lensa-apps-magic-avatars-violate-biometric-privacy-suit-says); [ClassAction.org](https://www.classaction.org/news/lensa-apps-magic-avatar-feature-illegally-captures-user-facial-geometry-class-action-claims)

**Russian law on biometrics (secondary legal blogs; verify with counsel)**
- **The laws.** 572-FZ (main part in force since 1 June 2023) governs biometric identification and authentication alongside art. 11 of 152-FZ. — [Selectel](https://selectel.ru/blog/how-to-deal-with-biometric-data/); [ITG Security](https://itgsecurity.ru/blog/how-to-avoid-fines-under-federal-law-no-572-when-working-with-biometrics)
- **Processing outside the EBS.** One source says art. 15 of 572-FZ prohibits organisations from processing or storing biometric data for identification or authentication outside the Unified Biometric System (EBS), with narrow exceptions. Another calls this contested. — [ITG Security](https://itgsecurity.ru/blog/how-to-avoid-fines-under-federal-law-no-572-when-working-with-biometrics); [iidx.ru](https://iidx.ru/blog/biometricheskie-personalnye-dannye-chto-eto-kak-reguliruyutsya-i-primenyayutsya-v-kyc/)

### Inferences
- **Technical architecture for a "stylized likeness" feature:**
  - One selfie, then an identity-preserving image-to-image model with a fixed, modest style prompt (hijab or kufi preserved, no body below the shoulders, no makeup or beauty enhancement).
  - A fixed seed and style per platform, so all avatars look consistently "illustrated".
  - An automated face-similarity check against the verification selfie, to stop someone using another person's face.
  - A visible "stylized" label.
- **Cost at $0.02–0.04 per image** with about 3–4 candidate generations per user: about $0.10–0.15 per onboarded user. This is an estimate.
- **Russian data law.** Sending Russian users' selfies to US-hosted APIs (OpenAI, fal, Replicate, Google) is a cross-border transfer of personal data, and possibly of biometric data. Self-hosting an open-weights pipeline (InstantID or PuLID on SDXL or FLUX) on Russian GPU infrastructure may be needed. This is an inference; data-localization and cross-border transfer rules were not researched.
- **Selfie-to-profile face matching** may count as "biometric identification" under 572-FZ. A manual moderator check by female staff (the Muzz model) without stored face templates may lower the risk. This needs Russian legal counsel.
- **Fiqh-sensitive risks of beautified or sexualized output** (the Lensa precedent) are serious for this audience. Disable "beauty" styles, keep negative prompts for revealing clothing, and moderate output before it is shown.

## 5. Onboarding UX (about 2-minute profile creation) and evidence on photo-free or blurred profiles

### Takeaway
Industry guidance favours:
- phone OTP or passwordless sign-in;
- about 3–5 sign-up steps before the user sees value;
- deferring long questionnaires (progressive profiling).

The supporting numbers are mostly unverified vendor claims. Evidence on photo-free profiles is thin and mixed:
- A 2024 peer-reviewed study (Tulane / *Information Systems Research*) found that ephemeral, controlled photos increased photo sharing, matches and conversations.
- Other secondary studies link high-quality photos to far more matches, and obscured photos to "untrustworthy" judgments.

Hiding photos therefore has an engagement cost that must be offset by other trust signals.

### Cited Findings
- **Sign-up flow.** Dating-app guidance calls the sign-up flow the single biggest drop-off point. It recommends email or phone sign-up of about 3–5 steps before users see matches, and deferring long questionnaires until after a first match. Its 85–90% completion figure is a vendor claim. — [AppMakers USA](https://appmakersla.com/blog/app-development/dating-app-design-tips/); [PG Dating Pro](https://www.datingpro.com/blog/essential-dating-app-features-for-a-successful-match-experience/)
- **Passwordless sign-in.** Passwordless phone or email codes and magic links are recommended. In markets like India, phone OTP is the expected primary method. — [AppMakers USA](https://appmakersla.com/blog/app-development/dating-app-design-tips/); [AllDayTech](https://alldaystech.com/guides/mobile-development/mobile-app-onboarding-ux-patterns-activation-retention)
- **Progressive profiling.** Collect essentials at sign-up and gather the rest later. A vendor claims this captures "about twice as much data without hurting sign-up rates". Use completion milestones and feature unlocks. Track completion at every step. — [Formbricks](https://formbricks.com/blog/user-onboarding-best-practices); [LowCode Agency](https://www.lowcode.agency/blog/mobile-onboarding-best-practices); [Ping Identity](https://pingidentity.com/en/resources/blog/post/frictionless-signup.html); [UXCam](https://uxcam.com/blog/10-apps-with-great-user-onboarding/)
- **Tulane study (Information Systems Research, 2024).** On a large Chinese dating app, ephemeral photos (which vanish after viewing) led users to share more photos, which produced more matches and more conversations. — [Tulane News](https://news.tulane.edu/pr/study-vanishing-photos-make-matches-multiply-dating-apps); [Phys.org](https://phys.org/news/2024-04-photos-dating-app.html)
- **Blurred-photo apps.** Taffy reveals the photo progressively, fully after about 10 messages, with conversation quality affecting the speed. — [Fox News](https://www.foxnews.com/lifestyle/new-dating-app-blurs-out-your-matchs-face-until-you-get-to-know-them). Tribal blurs photos for the first 72 hours of a connection. — [Vice](https://www.vice.com/en/article/this-dating-app-blurs-your-matches-faces-for-an-irl-love-is-blind/). Neither published outcome data.
- **Secondary photo studies.** An AURA analysis of 1.8M profiles linked high-quality photos to a 272% higher match rate than poor photos. A 2025 *Frontiers in Communication* study (n=389) found blurry or obscured photos prompted "untrustworthy" judgments. Both are secondary summaries; the originals were not verified. — [SwipeStats](https://www.swipestats.io/blog/anonymous-profile-picture)
- **Muslim-specific precedent.** Muzz originally blurred all photos. It now offers women opt-in blur, or default privacy per a newer blog; see section 3. Sunnah Match launched with no photos in its public showcase. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muzz_(dating_app)); [Sunnah Match](https://www.sunnahmatch.com/)

### Inferences
- **A possible 2-minute flow:**
  1. Phone OTP.
  2. Gender, age and city.
  3. Marriage intent and timeline.
  4. Madhhab or practice level (optional).
  5. Marital status (never married / divorced / widowed) and whether there are children.
  6. A choice of avatar (featureless default, or optional stylized).
  7. The user sees candidates.

  Defer the rest to progressive profiling: education, profession, nationality and languages, prayer and hijab, wali contact, the detailed "анкета", verification selfie. Gate chat (not browsing) behind selfie verification.
- **Offsetting photo-free trust costs.** If photos are hidden by default, the obscured-equals-untrustworthy effect (Frontiers 2025, secondary) has to be offset with other visible trust signals:
  - a "verified" badge (selfie checked by female moderators);
  - a "wali connected" badge;
  - richer text and voice;
  - mosque or community attestation.

  Voice intros are already used by Qaboolify, though voice may raise its own fiqh concerns for women. That is an inference; no source was checked.
- **A controlled-reveal model** (photo visible only to a chosen match, view-only, revocable) is closer to the Tulane "ephemeral photos" mechanism than permanent hiding is. The Tulane study suggests controlled, temporary visibility can increase willingness to share. Generalizing from a Chinese mainstream dating app to Russian Muslim users is uncertain.

### Gaps
- No rigorous study was found comparing engagement or retention for photo-free vs photo profiles, in Muslim or mainstream apps. "Slow dating" company statements with numbers were not found.
- No public Muzz or Hinge onboarding funnel metrics (time to complete, drop-off per step) were found.
- No Russian-market onboarding benchmarks were found (e.g. Mamba, VK Dating; VK Dating shut down in 2023, per my own background knowledge, not verified here).
- The search budget ran out before Nielsen Norman Group or Appcues sources could be retrieved.
