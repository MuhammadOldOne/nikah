## 1. Which dedicated websites and mobile apps exist for Russian-language nikah matchmaking? (URL, launch, owner, users, ratings, alive?)

### Takeaway
The market is fragmented and small-scale. No dominant Russian nikah product is visible. Players fall into four groups:
- A handful of long-running Russian-language websites, all with self-claimed audiences of roughly 20k–62k profiles: MuslimaNikah/LoveNikah, NikahPlus, Nikah.su, TatarNikah and NikahCity/marrymecity.
- A few small Russian-made apps, each with about 50 App Store ratings: Nikah Time, and HalalGuide's Halal Dating, now apparently Zawajy.
- Global apps (Muzz, Nikah.com) with Russian localisation but thin Russian audiences.
- General dating sites with religion filters (LovePlanet, LinkYou).

The most-funded and most-covered current entrant is **Zawajy** (Kazan IT-park, 2026). It claims about 20k users and plans an 85M ₽ raise.

## 2. Telegram channels, chats, bots and VK communities: size and flow

### Takeaway
Telegram is the de facto mass channel for Russian nikah matchmaking. It runs as a network of admin-run "агентства" that post anonymised profiles. Women post free and men pay roughly 200–500 ₽ per placement. Contacts or closed "VIP" channels are sold for up to about 7,600 ₽ ("безлимит"), and a "find a wife for you" service costs about 10,000 ₽. Reported channel sizes range from a few hundred to ~36–50k subscribers. VK was the main venue about 2017–2018, when Nikah.su's VK account had ~20k members. Today's VK groups found are small (~1k).

## 4. Mechanics of the major players: profile fields, photos, contact initiation, wali, moderation, verification, gender rules, monetization and prices

### Takeaway
The products split into three mechanic families:
1. **Swipe/like apps with Islamic overlays:** Muzz, Zawajy, Halal Dating. They offer wali or third-person chat, photo verification, aurat-covered photo rules or photo blur, and subscriptions for more likes, "see who liked you" and AI matching.
2. **Moderated "closed club" websites:** Nikah.su, NikahPlus, MuslimaNikah, TatarNikah. These feature manual profile approval, no-flirting and no-contact-exchange rules, guardian-only offline meetings, and internal coins or VIP.
3. **Admin-mediated Telegram agencies:** anonymous profiles, contact via admin, women free, men pay 200 ₽ – 10,000 ₽.

Published ruble prices for apps are scarce. Telegram and agency prices are the best documented.

### Inferences
- **Core trust gap:** paid admin-run Telegram channels with no accountability, scam risk, and men seeking secret or second marriages. Women and their families are the side most needing safety signals (verified identity, wali involvement, transparent pricing).
- **Liquidity gap:** global apps lack Russian users, and Russian apps have small bases. Both point to network-effect weakness that a Russian-first product must solve via a Telegram distribution funnel or partnerships.
- **Religious legitimacy as a growth lever:** theological ambivalence about online contact means endorsement from a muftiate or imams, plus wali-mediated design, may unlock users who avoid "dating apps".
