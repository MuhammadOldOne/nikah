# Online Muslim marriage matchmaking in Muslim-majority and nearby markets (excluding Russia and the Western diaspora apps), as of October 2026

*Method note: research done 30 Oct 2026 using web search across English, Russian, Uzbek, Indonesian/Malay, Turkish, Arabic, Persian and Bengali queries. Direct page fetching was blocked in this environment for most domains (thediplomat.com, dawn.com, restofworld.org, ordhekdeen.com, digitalbusiness.kz, madeinmalaysia.com.my and others failed DNS resolution), so the findings below rest on search-engine extracts/quotes of the cited pages, not full reads. Figures that platforms claim about themselves (users, marriages) are marked "self-reported". Older data are dated.*

## 1. Central Asia & Caucasus (Uzbekistan, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Tajikistan, Azerbaijan)

### Takeaway
The region runs on Telegram, not apps. The dominant model is the Uzbek "sovchilar" (matchmakers) channel: an admin posts anonymised profiles ("anketa") that come in through a form bot, and charges a small fee to post (reported at $1.5–7 in 2023). Some channels also sell paid entry to closed channels with contacts. Branded apps (Sovchi.app, SovchiApp, Vafo) are only starting to formalise this, and one already shows parents' phone numbers rather than the candidate's. There is almost no specific regulation. The state and religious bodies act only at the edges: Kazakhstan's muftiate tolerates sharia-compliant dating sites, a Kazakh court banned the general "Дайвинчик" dating bot, and Uzbekistan criminalises the polygamy these channels sometimes enable.

### Cited Findings
**Uzbekistan: Telegram "sovchilar" channels (core model)**
- Telegram dating groups titled "Sovchilar" (Matchmakers) were described in Jan 2023 as "reaching tens of thousands of subscribers nationwide", and some men use them to look for second wives. Polygamy is illegal: Criminal Code Art. 126 punishes it with a fine, correctional labour or up to 3 years in prison — [Daryo, Jan 2023](https://daryo.uz/en/2023/01/23/media-men-start-searching-for-second-wives-while-polygamy-prohibited-by-law-in-uzbekistan/); [Wikipedia: Polygamy in Uzbekistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polygamy_in_Uzbekistan)
- 2023 reporting says these platforms "serve one purpose – to post anonymous profiles of potential brides and grooms". One matchmaking Telegram page charged **$1.5–$7 to post a bride/groom profile across four Telegram pages**. A diaspora channel, "Sovchilar America", had almost 15,000 members — [The Diplomat, Mar 2023](https://thediplomat.com/2023/03/online-bride-and-prejudice-in-uzbek-society/) (via search extract. The article could not be fetched, and the $1.5–7 and 15k figures may come from this piece or from the Daryo item above.)
- A Tashkent channel ("ТАШКЕНТ СВАХИ / ЗНАКОМСТВА") had ~19,757 members and runs **separate contacts for grooms, for brides and "for 2nd marriage", plus a bot for filling in the questionnaire** — [tgramlink listing](https://tgramlink.com/ru/info/tashkent-matchmaking-marriage-agency-uzbekistan)
- The Uzbek-language "SOVCHILAR" channel sends people to a profile-submission bot and states "Elon pullik" (posting is paid) — [TGStat @sovchilik_znakomstva_yor_yor](https://uz.tgstat.com/en/channel/@sovchilik_znakomstva_yor_yor)
- Scale of the biggest channels (catalogue figures, date of snapshot unclear): one "Sovchilar" channel ~103K members — [telegram-store catalogue](https://uz.telegram-store.com/catalog/channels/sovchilar_sevishganlar_sovchilik). Smaller city channels run to a few thousand, e.g. "Toshkent Sovchilar" ~2,961 — [Telemetr](https://telemetr.io/en/channels/1310554735-toshkent_yor_yoruz_sovchilar). Other examples: "Sovchilar Toshkent" — [TGStat](https://uz.tgstat.com/en/channel/@sovchilar_poytaxt); "SOVCHILAR UZ" — [Telemetr](https://telemetr.me/content/sovchilar_tanishuv_nikoh_yoryor); "Nikah.com 💍 Совчилар Sovchilik Online" — [TGStat](https://uz.tgstat.com/fa/channel/@Nikah_com)
- Channels send price questions to admins or to a sample/price-list account ("Эълон бериш ва нархлар учун намуна"). Some channels say posting is free and others say it is paid. Many posts **publish applicants' phone numbers/Telegram handles openly** — [SOVCHI.UZ channel](https://t.me/s/Sovchi_uzz?before=4474); [TGStat Sovchilar](https://tgstat.ru/en/channel/@Sovchilar_Tanishuv_Sovchilik). - Cultural signal: actress Huvaydo's satire of "searching for a bride" went viral — [Zamin.uz](https://zamin.uz/en/culture/222356-actress-huvaydos-satire-on-searching-for-a-bride-rocks-the-internet-video.html)

- "Никях Киргизы Никах" has a closed channel for brides from Kazakhstan and Kyrgyzstan, "paid, only for serious brothers", with an admin for purchase — [TGStat](https://kg.tgstat.com/en/channel/@nikah_kirgiziya). - Tajikistan: "digital matchmaking has gained traction in recent years, with relatives posting ads on Facebook matchmaking groups" seeking "decent and well-mannered" partners — [Times of Central Asia](https://timesca.com/in-tajikistan-tradition-defines-the-ideal-bride-in-a-changing-world/)
- Older precedent (~2013): Tajikistan's first matchmaking agency in Khujand **required applicants to provide a clean bill of health and an official document verifying marital status** — [RFE/RL](https://www.rferl.org/a/tajikistan-first-matchmaking-agency/25027307.html)
- Legal context: in Tajikistan the marriage age is 18 (17 in exceptional cases by court). Kyrgyzstan has criminalised child and forced marriage — [RFE/RL](https://www.rferl.org/a/kyrgyzstan-tajikistan-underage-marriage-girls-women-rights/33467736.html)

**Azerbaijan / Telegram-native halal tools**

- Telegram-native halal formats: **Zawji App**, "halal Muslim matchmaking that runs inside Telegram" with wali oversight — [app.zawji.app](https://app.zawji.app/). The "Muslim Marriage" channel bot **auto-exchanges contacts only on mutual likes** (new, few users) — [t.me/s/muslimmarry](https://t.me/s/muslimmarry?before=16)

### Inferences
- The Central Asian Telegram model is a **classifieds business, not a matching product**. Revenue comes from (a) posting fees per anketa, (b) paid entry to closed "brides with contacts" channels, and (c) ads. It needs almost no tech: one form bot plus admins. Russian-speaking Central Asian migrants in Russia are likely already using the same RU/UZ/TJ/KG channels ("Агентство Никах" networks also cover Dagestan), so a Russian nikah site competes directly with these channels for the same audience.
- Weak points that a structured platform could fix: open publication of phone numbers and photos (privacy and consent risk), no verification of marital status (the second-wife/tokal demand is a real and legally sensitive segment in Uzbekistan, where polygamy is a crime), scams and prepayment risk, and data obtained from third parties without consent (the CABAR university-data case).
- SovchiApp's "parents' phone visible after acceptance" is a cheap, culturally native way to route contact to the family/wali instead of the candidate. The Khujand agency's health and marital-status certificates show that document-based vetting is an accepted norm.
- Sorting anketas by ethnicity (Kazakh, Uzbek, Tajik, etc.) is a real demand signal in these channels. A Russian platform will probably need ethnicity/nationality filters for Caucasus and Central Asian users.

### Gaps
- No verified current (2026) tgstat subscriber totals were retrievable for the largest sovchi channels; catalogue figures are undated. No so'm price list for posting or closed-channel access was found.
- No Uzbek government regulation specific to Telegram matchmaking, and no statement from the Muslim Board on it, was found. No Kun.uz/Gazeta.uz coverage surfaced.
- Azerbaijan: no local online elçi players identified. Kyrgyzstan/Tajikistan: no news coverage or regulation of Telegram nikah channels found.
- Sovchi.app / Vafo user numbers, pricing and funding were not found.

## 2. Indonesia & Malaysia (ta'aruf apps, mediators, state programmes)

### Takeaway
Indonesia has the most developed Sharia-procedural digital model. The ta'aruf process (CV/biodata exchange, then mediated Q&A, then nadzor (viewing), then khitbah (proposal)) is turned into product steps. Many services are **non-profit or donation-based foundations** that use mediators/ustadz, blur photos until CVs are approved, and ban direct chat. Their throughput is modest (hundreds of marriages). Malaysia has larger freemium apps with wali-as-chaperone features (Baituljannah). Since Aug 2025 it also has the first **state-run sharia-compliant matchmaking portal** (Terengganu, Portal Jodoh Darul Iman), which checks marital status against the state Islamic affairs department. Demand for it was high (2,048 sign-ups in ~6 weeks) but marriages were few (1 by July 2026).

### Cited Findings
**Indonesia: players and mechanics**
- **Ta'aruf Online Indonesia (TOI)** (Yayasan Jadi Baik Indonesia): a "private, CV-based system". **Photos are blurred until the CV is approved**, and **direct chat between ikhwan and akhwat is not allowed**. 23K+ downloads, rating 3.5/5 — [mwm.ai listing](https://mwm.ai/apps/ta-aruf-online-indonesia/1523496461). The App Store copy contrasts TOI with Instagram/WhatsApp matchmakers that "require payment before registration" and says it uses **trained intermediaries**. It claims 10,000 registrants and 20 marriages in 2019 (self-reported) — [App Store TOI](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/taaruf-online-indonesia/id1523496461)
- TOI process as documented academically: registration → CV → ta'aruf request → (mediated) chat → **nadzor** → khitbah. It works with partner mediators and **offers online nadzor via Zoom** — [Undip "Interaksi Online" journal](https://ejournal3.undip.ac.id/index.php/interaksi-online/article/download/33306/26599). A phenomenological study found men choose TOI because of work schedules, **mediation by a cleric** and a wider pool, and only one participant had married — [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/363339558_PERJALANAN_CINTAKU_SEBUAH_STUDI_FENOMENOLOGIS_TENTANG_PENGALAMAN_PENCARIAN_JODOH_PADA_PRIA_PENGGUNA_APLIKASI_TA'ARUF_ONLINE_INDONESIA); [UII thesis on TOI Semarang](https://dspace.uii.ac.id/handle/123456789/49134)
- **Rumah Taaruf myQuran** (since 2010). Steps: registration → biodata selection → **biodata exchange by email → Q&A by email moderated by an admin**. **One member is matched with only one other member at a time** (no parallel processes). **No direct communication until the family stage**. Offline meetings are accompanied by a **regional mediator**, while a central admin coordinates the online stage — [Proses Taaruf Online](https://www.rumahtaaruf.com/p/proses-taaruf-online.html); [Tanya Jawab](https://www.rumahtaaruf.com/p/tanya-jawab-rumahtaarufcom.html). Stats: >2,500 online processes, 533 offline, **128 married couples (as of 7 Sep 2025)** — [Catatan Proses](https://www.rumahtaaruf.com/p/catatan-proses-taaruf.html). A thesis gives ~6,400 online processes from 2010 to Jul 2024 and 129 marriages, so the figures conflict — [Unisma repository](https://repository.unisma.ac.id/handle/123456789/11493)
- **Ta'aruf Lalu Nikah (TLN)**: a non-profit. **Registration and ta'aruf submission are free** and the process is **accompanied by a facilitator**. Claims 242 married couples (self-reported) — [taaruflalunikah.id](https://taaruflalunikah.id/); a sharia-law study of TLN (UIN Jakarta, 2020) concluded that compliance depends on user intention and process — [UIN Jakarta repository](https://repository.uinjkt.ac.id/dspace/bitstream/123456789/74219/1/FATIMAH%20-%20FSH.pdf)
- **Taarufonline.id** lists 17 ustadz intermediaries, and a review says ~21 — [taarufonline.id](https://taarufonline.id/); [landingspage.net review](https://landingspage.net/website-taaruf/)
- **Taaruf ID** (taarufapp.id) **sorts candidates by GPS proximity** and was updated June 2025 — [taarufapp.id](https://taarufapp.id/); [Uptodown](https://taaruf-id.en.uptodown.com/android). **Taaruf** (Infinet Media Solusi, taaruf.com) claims >1M users (unverified), with releases into Mar 2026 — [Uptodown](https://taaruf.en.uptodown.com/android); [taaruf.com](https://taaruf.com/). **Taaruf Online Sakinah**: CV → submit ta'aruf → chat, with a **subscription fee** — [taarufonline.com](https://taarufonline.com/). Others: [Hijra Taaruf](https://hijrataaruf.com/en/), [Mediataaruf](https://mediataaruf.com/)
- **CV Taarufku**: not a matching service but a **ta'aruf CV builder with AI and 7 templates**, which shows the standardised "CV ta'aruf" is a product in its own right — [cvtaarufku.id](https://cvtaarufku.id/)
- Academic framing: digital ta'aruf relies on a **third-party mediator** and a "flexible nadzor" (online or offline, always mediated) — [JICL, UNIDA Gontor](https://jicl.journal.unida.gontor.ac.id/index.php/jicl/article/download/74/30); [ResearchGate "Transformasi Makna Ta'aruf di Era Digital"](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/352851901_Transformasi_Makna_Ta'aruf_di_Era_Digital). A 2022 case study of fraud on Muzz in Indonesia analyses it as gharar and urges developers to improve systems — [Minhaj journal](https://jurnal.iaibafa.ac.id/index.php/minhaj/article/download/minhaj_juli2022_4/minhaj_juli2022_4)
- No Kemenag (Ministry of Religious Affairs) or MUI fatwa/endorsement of ta'aruf apps was found (search across app listings and DSN-MUI app) — [search context: DSN-MUI fatwa app](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=aw.sc.fatwa&hl=en_US)

**Malaysia: players and state programme**
- **Portal Jodoh Darul Iman (PJDI), Terengganu**: run by **Yayasan Pembangunan Keluarga Terengganu (YPKT)**, a state family development foundation, and launched in early Aug 2025 (sources say 6, 7 or 8 Aug). It is open to **Muslim Terengganu residents who are single, divorced or widowed**. Age limit is 18+ per the official page, though one listing says 21+. **Marital status is verified with JHEAT (state Islamic Affairs Department) to exclude already-married people** — [YPKT official page](https://www.ypkt.gov.my/ms/component/content/article/8-program-dan-perkhidmatan/71-jodoh-darul-iman?Itemid=117); [Kosmo, 6 Aug 2025](https://www.kosmo.com.my/2025/08/06/portal-jodoh-darul-iman-peluang-bujang-duda-janda-dapat-pasangan/); [Sinar Harian "patuh syariah"](https://www.sinarharian.com.my/article/742183/edisi/terengganu/cari-jodoh-patuh-syariah-menerusi-portal-jodoh-darul-iman)
- PJDI had **2,048 registrations by 16 Sep 2025, against an initial target of 500** — [TRDI](https://www.trdi.my/portal-jodoh-darul-iman-laku-keras-lebih-2000-cari-pasangan-dalam-sebulan); [TRDI target 500](https://www.trdi.my/sasar-500-individu-bujang-daftar-portal-jodoh). By ~15 Jul 2026 it had **united 1 couple, with ~10 more expected to marry that year** — [TRDI](https://www.trdi.my/portal-jodoh-terengganu-berjaya-satukan-pasangan-10-lagi-dijangka-kahwin-tahun-ini). It was framed by the PAS-led state as a response to the late-marriage trend — [Berita PAS](https://berita.pas.org.my/portal-jodoh-darul-iman-tangani-trend-lewat-kahwin/). Described as the first state-led Muslim matchmaking app in Malaysia, built by Top IT Industries Sdn Bhd — [madeinmalaysia.com.my](https://madeinmalaysia.com.my/terengganu-dating-app-jodoh-darul-iman/)
- **Baituljannah**: focuses on Ahlus Sunnah wal Jamaah. Claims >1,000,000 candidates registered from Malaysia, Singapore and Brunei (self-reported). **Two-stage verification**: an SMS code, then **a selfie holding an in-app code handwritten on paper**. **Users can register a guardian/close relative as "chaperone" in the message section during the ta'aruf session** — [Google Play Baituljannah](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.baituljannah&hl=en); [SourceForge listing](https://sourceforge.net/app/baituljannah-jodoh-muslim/android/). It is freemium with monthly premium plans, and part of the fee reportedly goes to charity. Marriage claims conflict (20,000+ vs 30,000+ couples) — [Tracxn](https://tracxn.com/d/companies/baituljannah/__m9X15xQGwP_yesLg1JIbPXgQN_VABvk4lPmEnwhf0h0). Registered users rose more than 85% during the pandemic MCO — [Malaysiakini announcement](https://www.malaysiakini.com/announcement/589866)
- **TillJannah.my**: a "Malaysian Muslim Dating Portal since 2016" where an **admin manually checks and approves profiles within 24h** — [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.app2.tilljannah&hl=en_US). **PlanB**: filters every candidate and **monitors users during ta'aruf sessions** — [planbcarijodoh.com](https://planbcarijodoh.com/). **Cintaku**: free Muslim matchmaking app — [cintaku.com](https://cintaku.com/)
- **Dil Ka Rishta** (Pakistani) also sells in Malaysia, at RM69.90/month Gold and RM299.90 lifetime — [App Store MY](https://apps.apple.com/my/app/dil-ka-rishta/id1501708912)
- No JAKIM-run matchmaking programme was found (searched).

### Inferences
- The Indonesian non-profit model (free or donation-based, a mediator in every conversation, one-at-a-time matching) maximises sharia legitimacy but produces **very low conversion**: Rumah Taaruf reports ~128 marriages from 2,500–6,400 processes over 15 years. The commercial lesson is to keep the *procedure* (CV, then mediated Q&A, then nadzor with family) and **charge for the mediator/facilitator stage** rather than for browsing.
- "One active ta'aruf at a time" is a distinctive, easy-to-copy mechanic. It signals seriousness and prevents "collecting" contacts. It could be a premium "serious mode" or the default.
- Blurring photos until mutual CV approval (TOI) and the wali-as-chaperone inside chat (Baituljannah) are productised answers to the photo and khalwa problem.
- Terengganu's PJDI shows that **verification against official marital-status records** is the trust feature religious authorities care about. In Russia, the nearest equivalents are a voluntary upload of a passport marriage stamp or a ZAGS certificate, or imam/mosque attestation.
- PJDI's 2,048 sign-ups but only 1 marriage after ~11 months suggests the state portal lacks active mediation and follow-up. Registration is easy and conversion is the hard part, which is a cautionary note for any platform.

### Gaps
- No independent user/MAU data for Indonesian ta'aruf apps; all numbers are self-reported.
- Pricing for Baituljannah premium, Taaruf ID and Taaruf Online Sakinah was not found.
- No Kemenag/MUI or JAKIM/state religious council position on matchmaking apps was found, except Terengganu (YPKT/JHEAT). Whether other states (e.g. Selangor, Kelantan) run similar portals was not verified.

## 3. Turkey

### Takeaway
No state or Diyanet-run matchmaking platform was found. The state's 2025 "Year of the Family" (Aile Yılı) pushes marriage through **financial support** (e-Devlet interest-free marriage loans, municipal free wedding halls/nikah/çeyiz) and premarital education, not matchmaking. Diyanet-linked guidance allows meeting online for marriage within adab limits, but its 2023 family book called video calls and long messaging between fiancés impermissible. The private market consists of many small "evlilik ilanları" (marriage-ad) sites and a few Islamic-niche ones (Helal Yuva, religious-nikah sites, Sadece Evlilik), plus the global apps.

### Cited Findings
- 2025 Aile Yılı: **109 municipalities offer 168 supports in 16 categories** to young couples, including free nikah, wedding halls and çeyiz (trousseau) — [Ministry of Family (aile.gov.tr)](https://www.aile.gov.tr/haberler/aile-yili-kapsaminda-belediyelerden-genc-ciftlere-destek/)
- "Evlenecek Gençlerin Desteklenmesi Projesi" (support for young people marrying) was opened nationwide via **e-Devlet** — [aile.gov.tr](https://www.aile.gov.tr/btgmd/haberler/evlenecek-genclerin-desteklenmesi-projesi-tum-turkiye-icin-e-devlet-uzerinden-hizmete-acildi/). Couples reportedly must commit to attending the ministry's pre- and post-marriage education/counselling — [Fayn Press](https://www.fayn.press/2025-yili-bir-kucuk-aile-meselesi/)
- Diyanet's 2023 family book (distributed free) states that **video calls via mobile phone or long messaging between fiancés are not permissible** (caiz değil), nor is any communication that breaches privacy/morality — [Ekmek ve Gül](https://ekmekvegul.net/gundem/diyanetin-aile-kitabi-nisanlilarin-goruntulu-konusmasi-caiz-degil)

## 4. Middle East / Gulf / North Africa (incl. Iran)

### Takeaway
Three models coexist. (1) **Commercial apps with an Arab "khattaba" layer**: AlKhattaba (Saudi) uses a long compatibility questionnaire, selfie verification and a paid human khattaba tier. (2) **Licensed charities as matchmakers**: Saudi family/marriage associations registered with the HR & Social Development ministry run confidential "tawfiq" (matching) services with no direct contact between parties, some now on platforms with "smart recommendation + human supervision" (Sakann). Hamdam requires ID verification and a psychological test and provides consultants who introduce the families and follow the couple for years. Its cumulative output is only a few thousand marriages. Egypt and the Maghreb run largely on traditional khatba plus Facebook groups and low-end chat apps. Hawaya (Match Group) appears to have closed.

- The Gulf "khattaba" pattern is **software for discovery plus a human for closing**. The paid human-matchmaker tier is a natural premium SKU (AlKhattaba, Dil Ka Rishta VIP, NikahForever Assisted all converge on it).
- The Saudi charity-association model (confidential, no direct contact, eligibility checks, under a government-registered NGO) is the closest analogue to a **mosque/muftiate-affiliated service in Russia**. A partnership with a regional DUM could lend legitimacy in a similar way.
- Iran shows the ceiling of state/ideological matchmaking. Heavy vetting (ID + psych test) and family-centred consultant mediation produce a very low failure rate but tiny volume (single-digit thousands of marriages over ~15 years, and only 6,500 verified out of 150k installs). Heavy verification drastically cuts the active pool.

## 5. South Asia (Pakistan, India, Bangladesh): the matrimony model

### Takeaway
South Asia runs on **"matrimony", not dating**. Profiles are often created and run by parents, built around a standardised **biodata** (education, profession, family, sect, location). Browsing is free, and the money comes from **paid contact unlocking**: per-contact "connections" (OrdhekDeen, Bangladesh) or time-based subscriptions (Dil Ka Rishta, NikahForever, Matrimony.com). An **assisted/VIP human-matchmaker tier** sells at many times the subscription price. Offline "rishta aunties"/ghotoks charge Rs50,000–200,000 per match in Pakistan and are digitising via WhatsApp. India's market leader, Matrimony.com, is flat at ~1M paid subscriptions a year with shrinking volume.

## 6. Cross-regional patterns and lessons for a Russian nikah website

### Takeaway
Across all regions, the Sharia-conscious online model differs from dating along five axes:
1. **Who acts**: parents/wali create or co-manage the profile, or receive the contact.
2. **What is shown**: a standardised CV/biodata/anketa, with photos hidden or blurred until mutual interest.
3. **How contact happens**: through a mediator, a chaperone in chat, or a direct hand-off to family, not open chat.
4. **Who closes**: a human matchmaker (sovchi, khattaba, rishta aunty, ghotok, ustadz/mediator, state consultant).
5. **What is charged**: posting fees, closed-channel access, per-contact unlocks, subscriptions and premium human matchmaking.

Verification is the weakest link everywhere. The best examples tie it to official marital-status records (Terengganu), documents (Khujand agency), ID plus psych test (Iran) or selfie-with-code (Baituljannah, AlKhattaba).

### Cited Findings
- **Guardian/parent as actor**:
  - Bangladesh platforms let parents create and manage biodatas and give guardian phone numbers — [BibahoBangla](https://bibahobangla.com/); [NoGhotok](https://noghotok.com/en/)
  - Uzbekistan's SovchiApp reveals **parents' phone numbers on acceptance** and runs parent-supervised chat — [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.sovchi.app&hl=en)
  - Malaysia's Baituljannah adds a **guardian chaperone** to the chat — [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.baituljannah&hl=en)
  - Pakistani apps send weekly chat transcripts to a relative — [Arab News](https://www.arabnews.com/node/2575616/lifestyle)
- **CV/biodata format**:
  - Indonesian "CV ta'aruf" (even a dedicated AI CV builder) — [CV Taarufku](https://cvtaarufku.id/)
  - Bangladeshi "biodata" with upazila-level location — [OrdhekDeen](https://ordhekdeen.com/en)

### Inferences
These are adaptable mechanics for a Russian nikah website, prioritised by evidence of use:
1. **Telegram-first distribution plus a structured web/app back end.** CIS Muslims already search in Telegram sovchi/nikah channels. A form-bot intake that feeds a moderated anketa database (with Russian/Uzbek/Tajik/Kyrgyz language and ethnicity filters) meets users where they are. Telegram mini-app competitors already exist (Zawji).
2. **Standardised "анкета/CV для никаха"** modelled on the ta'aruf CV and the South Asian biodata: religiosity, madhhab, prayer, hijab/beard, marriage history, children, readiness to relocate, family info, wali contact. Optionally offer a CV builder as a free hook (cf. CV Taarufku).
3. **Wali-routed contact**: on mutual interest, release the wali/parent's contact (SovchiApp, NoGhotok), or add the wali as chat chaperone (Baituljannah). Hide or blur photos until mutual CV approval (TOI).
4. **Monetise intent, not chatting**: pay-per-contact credits (OrdhekDeen) plus subscription, with a premium **"сваха/посредник" (mediator) tier** at roughly 10x price (Dil Ka Rishta VIP, AlKhattaba, NikahForever Assisted). Also offer a mediated first video meeting or nadzor (Helal Yuva, TOI Zoom).
5. **"Serious mode"**: one active ta'aruf at a time (Rumah Taaruf), which reduces contact-hoarding and harassment.
6. **Verification as the brand**: selfie-with-code, plus optional document check of marital status (passport stamp or divorce certificate). A clear polygamy policy is needed, because second-wife demand is real in the CIS but legally fraught: polygamy is criminal in Uzbekistan, and in Russia nikah without ZAGS has no civil effect.
7. **Institutional legitimacy**: partner with a muftiate or mosques, as Saudi charities run matching under a ministry and Kazakhstan's DUMK accepts sharia-compliant sites. Optionally add premarital courses, as Turkey's state support requires.

Caution: the non-profit, heavily mediated models (Rumah Taaruf, Hamdam, PJDI) show high legitimacy but low throughput, so mediation should be a premium accelerator, not a bottleneck for everyone.
